Frequently Asked Questions

How many Museums for All locations are in Oregon?
There are 32 participating Museums for All locations listed in Oregon, each offering free or reduced admission to EBT/SNAP cardholders.
What is Museums for All?
Museums for All is a national access program that encourages families receiving food assistance (SNAP) benefits to visit museums for free or a reduced fee. Participating museums offer free admission or admission for $3 or less per person (up to four people) upon presentation of a SNAP EBT card.
Who qualifies for free or reduced museum admission?
Anyone with a valid SNAP EBT (Electronic Benefits Transfer) card qualifies. Simply present your EBT card, usually along with a photo ID, at the participating museum's admissions desk.
How much does admission cost with an EBT card?
At participating Museums for All locations, admission is free or $3 or less per person for up to four people, depending on the museum.
Is this the official Museums for All website?
No. This is a free, independent, open-source map and is not affiliated with Museums for All, the Institute of Museum and Library Services (IMLS), the Association of Children's Museums (ACM), or SNAP/EBT. Always confirm current admission details with the museum directly.
